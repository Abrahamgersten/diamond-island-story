A Few Words About Diamond Island

"Diamond Island" is an adventure story written especially for elementary school kids. It's the story of Moshe, a father struggling to provide for his family, who sets off on a surprising journey to a faraway, glittering island - and along the way learns an important lesson about what truly matters in life.

12 chapters, one at a time - just like Moshe himself lived them, step by step. Chapter One is ready now with full English narration, read in a voice specially created for this story - and more chapters are on their way!
